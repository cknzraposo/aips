# Data Sources — NZ AI Economy Simulator
*Compiled: 2026-04-11*

## Primary NZ Data Sources

### Government & Official Statistics
1. **Stats NZ**
   - Business Operations Survey (digital tech adoption)
   - ICT workforce statistics
   - National accounts (GDP by sector)
   - URL: stats.govt.nz

2. **MBIE (Ministry of Business, Innovation & Employment)**
   - NZ AI Strategy & State of Play
   - Space sector reports
   - Science & Technology indicators
   - URL: mbie.govt.nz

3. **Callaghan Innovation**
   - R&D grant recipients
   - Innovation ecosystem reports
   - Startup funding data
   - URL: callaghaninnovation.govt.nz

4. **NZ Productivity Commission**
   - Economic modelling studies
   - Productivity measurement reports
   - Technology & growth research
   - URL: productivity.govt.nz

### Industry Bodies
5. **Tech NZ (formerly NZTech)**
   - Annual tech sector report (GDP, employment, exports)
   - Most comprehensive industry-wide data
   - URL: technewzealand.org.nz

6. **AI Forum NZ**
   - Annual AI Productivity Report (adoption rates, barriers, impact)
   - Third report (2024) most recent
   - URL: aiforum.org.nz

7. **New Zealand AI (industry coalition)**
   - AI adoption surveys
   - Skills gap analysis
   - URL: newzealand.ai

### Research & Think Tanks
8. **BERL (Business & Economic Research Ltd)**
   - Space economy report (2019)
   - Economic impact studies
   - URL: berl.co.nz

9. **NZIER (NZ Institute of Economic Research)**
   - Economic forecasts
   - Sector analysis
   - URL: nzier.org.nz

## OECD & International Data Sources

### OECD
10. **OECD AI Policy Observatory**
    - Country profiles (including NZ)
    - AI policy tracker
    - International comparisons
    - URL: oecd.ai

11. **OECD Digital Economy Outlook**
    - Published annually/biennially
    - Digital economy indicators by country
    - URL: oecd.org/digital/

12. **OECD SME & Entrepreneurship Outlook**
    - SME digital adoption
    - Cross-country analysis
    - URL: oecd.org/industry/smes/

### Global Research & Consulting
13. **McKinsey Global Institute**
    - AI impact studies
    - Future of work reports
    - Sector productivity analysis
    - URL: mckinsey.com/mgi

14. **PWC / Deloitte / Accenture**
    - AI impact projections
    - Industry surveys
    - Economic modelling
    - Various URLs (reports often gated)

15. **World Bank**
    - Digital economy indicators
    - Development data (global)
    - URL: worldbank.org

16. **World Economic Forum (WEF)**
    - Future of Jobs reports
    - Global space economy projections
    - Technology governance
    - URL: weforum.org

### Venture Capital & Investment Data
17. **CB Insights**
    - Global AI funding tracker
    - Startup landscape
    - URL: cbinsights.com (subscription)

18. **Crunchbase**
    - Startup funding data
    - NZ tech company profiles
    - URL: crunchbase.com (freemium)

19. **PitchBook**
    - PE/VC data
    - Market analysis
    - URL: pitchbook.com (subscription)

## Gaps & Additional Sources Needed

### Identified gaps
1. **Granular sector-level AI adoption** — Need Stats NZ Business Ops Survey detailed results
2. **NZ AI specialist workforce count** — No authoritative source; need to triangulate (LinkedIn, Immigration NZ, uni grads)
3. **Regional variation within NZ** — Auckland vs Wellington vs regions; limited data
4. **Longitudinal firm-level data** — Track same firms over time with/without AI; need research partnership
5. **NZ AI export revenue** — Which companies scaling globally? Revenue data sparse

### Potential additional sources
- **Contact Stats NZ directly** — Request microdata access for Business Ops Survey
- **AI Forum NZ** — Request granular data from 2024 survey
- **NZTE (NZ Trade & Enterprise)** — Export data for tech companies
- **Angel Association NZ / Venture Capital Association** — NZ VC funding data
- **Research collaboration** — Partner with uni (e.g., VUW School of Economics) for data access & modelling
