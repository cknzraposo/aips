### AI Adoption Rates (NZ) - PROVENANCE ANALYSIS COMPLETE

⚠️ The commonly cited "44%" is NOT an adoption rate. See `provenance-analysis.md` for full analysis.

The 44% comes from KPMG/UoM (2025) - a public trust metric ("benefits outweigh risks"), not business adoption.

Actual adoption figures in circulation:
- NZIER/Spark QSBO (2024): **32%** - SMEs with plans to evaluate/invest (nationally representative)
- Datacom (2023): **48%** - larger businesses using AI (200 senior leaders)
- Datacom (2024): **67%** - same methodology, one year later
- AI Forum (Sep 2024): **~67%** - self-selected network
- AI Forum (Mar 2025): **82%** - self-selected network
- AI Forum (Aug 2025): **87%** - self-selected network
- KPMG/UoM (2025): **69%** - workers using AI regularly (global survey)

The 2.7x gap (32% → 87%) reflects different populations, definitions, and selection bias.
MBIE's NZ AI Strategy (July 2025) cites contradictory figures without reconciling them.

**Source quality:** ⭐⭐⭐ (multiple sources exist, but methodologically incoherent - reconciling this IS the contribution)

## What We Don't Know (Data Gaps)

### 1. Granular Sector-Level AI Adoption (NZ)
❌ **Gap:** AI Forum NZ report gives overall 44%, but limited breakdown by industry  
❌ **Need:** Manufacturing vs finance vs healthcare vs agriculture vs public sector adoption rates  
❌ **Why it matters:** Different sectors have different productivity potential and barriers

**Mitigation:** Contact AI Forum NZ directly for detailed survey data; check Stats NZ Business Operations Survey for tech adoption questions

---

### 2. NZ AI/ML Specialist Workforce Size
❌ **Gap:** No authoritative count of AI/ML specialists in NZ  
❌ **Estimate:** ~2,000-3,000 (inferred from 119k tech workforce)  
❌ **Shortage:** 5,000-10,000 additional specialists needed for high adoption scenario (extrapolated from OECD data)

**Mitigation:** Triangulate from:
- University CS grad numbers (Auckland, VUW, Canterbury, Otago, Waikato)
- Immigration NZ skills visa data (AI/ML roles)
- LinkedIn job postings + Economic Graph data
- Contact universities for placement tracking

---

### 3. NZ VC Investment in AI Startups
❌ **Gap:** NZ VC data fragmented, not AI-specific  
❌ **Estimate:** <$100M annually (vs $50-80B globally = <0.5% share)  
❌ **Need:** Which NZ AI companies scaling globally? Revenue data?

**Mitigation:** Contact:
- Angel Association NZ / NZVCA for investment data
- NZTE for tech export data
- Crunchbase/PitchBook for startup funding (subscription required)
- Callaghan Innovation for R&D grant recipients

---

### 4. Longitudinal Firm-Level Productivity Data
❌ **Gap:** No tracking of same NZ firms over time (AI adopters vs non-adopters)  
❌ **Need:** Empirical productivity gains from AI adoption in NZ context  
❌ **Why it matters:** OECD estimates are global/cross-country; NZ context may differ

**Mitigation:** 
- Request Stats NZ Business Longitudinal Database access (research partnership required)
- Partner with university economics department (e.g., VUW) for data access & analysis
- Run own survey of NZ businesses (expensive, time-consuming)

---

### 5. Regional Variation Within NZ
❌ **Gap:** Auckland vs Wellington vs regions - AI adoption patterns unknown  
❌ **Why it matters:** Policy interventions may need geographic targeting (e.g., regional skills programs)

**Mitigation:**
- Stats NZ may have regional breakdowns in Business Ops Survey
- AI Forum NZ survey might have respondent location (request raw data)
- Proxy: Tech sector employment by region (already know Auckland dominates)

---

### 6. Labour Market Impact (NZ-Specific)
❌ **Gap:** OECD estimates 10-30% of jobs have >50% automatable tasks, but not NZ breakdown  
❌ **Need:** Which NZ industries/occupations most vulnerable? Net job displacement vs creation?

**Mitigation:**
- Apply OECD methodology to NZ occupation data (Stats NZ)
- Review: NZ Productivity Commission "Technology & the Future of Work" research
- Contact: Future of Work Commission, if still active

---

### 7. NZ AI Export Success Stories
❌ **Gap:** Which NZ AI companies scaling globally? Revenue, employment, impact?  
❌ **Examples we know:** Soul Machines, Ika, Melodics (but limited public data)

**Mitigation:**
- NZTE export data
- Tech NZ member profiles
- Direct outreach to companies (interview-based research)

## Recommendations for Simulator Development

### Phase 1: Build with what we have (Q2 2026)
- Use existing high-confidence data (NZ baseline, OECD comparatives, global productivity estimates)
- Clearly document assumptions & data quality limitations
- Build sensitivity analysis into model (how much do results change if key parameters vary?)
- Focus on directional insights, not precise predictions

### Phase 2: Refine with better data (Q3-Q4 2026)
- Incorporate sector-level adoption rates once obtained
- Validate productivity multipliers with NZ experts/case studies
- Add regional dimension once data available
- Test model against 2019-2024 actual outcomes (backtest)

### Phase 3: Iterate based on stakeholder feedback (2027)
- Present to AI Forum NZ, MBIE, Productivity Commission
- Refine based on expert input
- Add complexity only where it improves decision-making (avoid over-fitting)

---

## Key Insight: Data Quality is "Good Enough" to Start

**Bottom line:** We have sufficient data to build a credible first-cut model. The gaps are real, but they're not blockers. Start with what we know, document limitations, and improve iteratively as better data becomes available.

The simulator's value is in **scenario exploration** (what if adoption accelerates? what if skills gap persists?), not precise forecasting. For that purpose, current data quality is adequate.
